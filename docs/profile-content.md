### 01 · Survey & Engineering  (slug: survey-engineering)
Summary items: Differential GPS survey · Underground detection

**Surveying & underground detection** (subsection: survey-underground-detection)
- Differential GPS survey — Survey control and setting-out by differential GPS on site.
- Underground detection — Cable and pipe locating before any excavation starts.
- Accurate from day one — Surveying teams support design, setting-out and as-built checks.

### 02 · Earthworks  (slug: earthworks)
Summary items: Excavation, cut & fill · Backfill & compaction · Right of way construction

**Excavation, cut & fill** (subsection: excavation-cut-fill)
- Cutting & filling — Excavators, loaders and dump trucks shape the formation to level.
- Backfill & compaction — Backfill is compacted in layers and verified by field compaction testing.
- Own fleet — Plant works under our own supervision. See the fleet list for details.

**Right of way construction** (subsection: right-of-way)
- Corridor formation — Road corridor cleared, cut and graded to the design alignment.
- Heavy plant — Dozers, graders and rollers work the formation under our supervision.

### 03 · Road Works  (slug: road-works)
Summary items: Road construction & double dressing · Asphalt cutting & paving · Concrete channels, signage & fencing

**Road construction & double dressing** (subsection: road-construction)
- Road construction — Formation, base layers and compaction with our own roller fleet.
- Double dressing — Two-course surface treatment: binder sprayed, aggregate chips rolled in, twice.
- Compaction — HAMM and BOMAG steel rollers plus a HAMM sheepfoot roller.

**Asphalt cutting & paving** (subsection: asphalt-cutting-paving)
- Asphalt cutting — Clean cut lines in existing roads using a cutter machine.
- Asphalt paving — Asphalt surfacing laid and rolled on road sections.
- Protected works — Cones and barrier tape keep the public clear of open works.

**Concrete channels, signage & fencing** (subsection: channels-signage-fencing)
- Concrete kerbs & channels — Concrete channels and kerbs cast beside the carriageway.
- Marker posts & safety signs — Warning and PPE signs installed on posts at work areas.
- Site fencing — Fence installation to secure and protect the works area.

### 04 · Slopes & Drainage  (slug: slopes-drainage)
Summary items: Hill reslope & gabion walls · Riprap & drainage ditches · Geotextile, lining & rock shield

**Slope remediation & gabion walls** (subsection: reslope-gabion)
- Re-slope & re-scale — Hillsides cut back and scaled to the design slope for a stable corridor.
- Gabion wall construction — Stepped gabion walls built to retain cut slopes and structures.
- Heavy fleet — Excavators and loaders from our own fleet work the face.

**Riprap & drainage ditches** (subsection: riprap-ditches)
- Geotextile & riprap — Geotextile laid under stone riprap to resist erosion.
- Re-slope side ditches — Side ditches re-sloped and stone-lined to carry runoff clear of the road.

**Geotextile, lining & rock shield** (subsection: geotextile-lining)
- Geotextile & rock shield — Geotextile and rock-shield protection installed around buried pipelines.
- Waste water construction — Geotextile and liner laid and compacted in waste water ponds.

### 05 · Pipelines & Utilities  (slug: pipelines-utilities)
Summary items: Trench excavation · Polyethylene water pipe · Hydrostatic testing & manholes

**Trench excavation** (subsection: trench-excavation)
- Utility & drainage trenches — Open trenches cut to line and depth for utilities and drainage.
- Live roads — Trenches along live roads, protected with cones and barrier tape.

**Polyethylene water pipe installation** (subsection: pe-water-pipe)
- Laying — Polyethylene water pipe laid in the trench to line and level.
- Welding — Pipe lengths welded to form continuous water mains.
- Along the corridor — Installation runs beside live roads and across open ground.

**Joint connection & hydrostatic test** (subsection: joints-hydrostatic-test)
- Valves & joint connection — Valves, tees and fittings connected along the water line.
- Hydrostatic test — Completed lines are pressure-tested before acceptance.

**Manhole construction** (subsection: manholes)
- Formwork & concrete — Chambers cast in timber formwork and finished to drawing.
- Protective coating — Chamber walls coated before backfilling.
- Cover slabs — Cover slabs with access openings fitted on each manhole.

### 06 · Concrete & Structures  (slug: concrete-structures)
Summary items: Concrete works · Formwork & rebar · Tower formwork

**Concrete, formwork & reinforcement** (subsection: concrete-formwork-rebar)
- Concrete works — Slab pours finished with power trowels, plus channels and kerbs.
- Formwork — Timber and steel formwork for walls, set out and braced on site.
- Rebar installation — Reinforcement fixed to drawing before every pour.

**Tower formwork & rebar** (subsection: tower-formwork-rebar)
- Slab formwork & shoring — Formwork and propping set out for tall tower slabs.
- Rebar for towers — Reinforcement installed to drawing before each tower pour.

### 07 · Oil & Gas  (slug: oil-gas)

**Masonry: block & brick** (subsection: masonry)

## 12. How We Work (4 steps)

1. **Consultation & Estimate** — We walk the site, scope the work, and return a fully costed proposal.
2. **Design & Planning** — Drawings, method statements, and a programme resourced before it is promised.
3. **Groundwork & Build** — Earthworks, structure and envelope, executed to the approved drawing.
4. **Inspection & Handover** — QA/QC sign-off, snagging closed out, and a documented handover.
